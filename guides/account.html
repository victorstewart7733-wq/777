Account & login access

General sign-in, password reset and account security information. Follow the general steps below to find the right next step for your question.

What to do

Confirm the sign-in details
Check that you are using the email address or phone number associated with your account.
Use the official password reset
Choose the password recovery option on the official app or website and follow the verification steps.
Check your messages
Look for the expected verification or reset message, including spam or junk folders.
Secure your account
Use a unique password and do not share verification codes. Contact official support if you suspect unauthorized access.
For security, do not send passwords or one-time codes to this website or to anyone who contacts you unexpectedly.
