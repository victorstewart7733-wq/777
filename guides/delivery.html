Late or missing delivery

What to check if an order is delayed or marked delivered but you cannot find it. Follow the general steps below to find the right next step for your question.

What to do

Check the latest arrival estimate
Open the order in your account and review the most recent delivery estimate and status.
Review delivery messages
Look for messages, calls or in-app notifications about delays, substitutions or drop-off details.
Check the delivery location
If marked delivered, check the selected drop-off area and any delivery photo or instructions shown in the order.
Contact official order support
If the order is still missing or the delivery details are incorrect, report the issue through the order's Help option in the official app or website.
