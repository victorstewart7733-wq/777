Missing, damaged or wrong items

General guidance for reviewing item issues, substitutions and order adjustments. Follow the general steps below to find the right next step for your question.

What to do

Open the completed order
Find the relevant order in your account and review the item list and receipt.
Identify the item issue
Note whether an item is missing, damaged, incorrect or an unexpected replacement.
Use the order issue flow
Choose the matching issue in the official app or website and follow the prompts.
Keep useful details
If requested, provide a clear description and relevant photos of the item or packaging.
Eligibility, reporting windows and available resolutions are determined by the retailer's current policies and the details of your order.
