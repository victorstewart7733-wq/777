Order cancellations

Review cancellation options and the official next steps. Follow the general steps below to find the right next step for your question.

What to do

Open the order
Find the order in your account and review its current status.
Look for cancellation options
If cancellation is available, use the control shown in the official app or website.
Review any displayed terms
Check any notice about fees, timing or possible adjustments before confirming.
If no option appears
Contact official Instacart support promptly and explain what you need.
Cancellation availability and any related charges depend on order status and current retailer policies.
