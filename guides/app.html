App & technical issues

Basic troubleshooting for app loading, updates and connection issues. Follow the general steps below to find the right next step for your question.

What to do

Check your connection
Switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data to see whether the issue is related to the internet connection.
Restart the app
Close the app fully, reopen it and try the action again.
Check for updates
Visit your device's official app store and install any available app update.
Restart your device
If the issue continues, restart your phone and try again.
Use official support
If you still cannot access your account or an order, contact Instacart through its official Help Center.
Menu names and app features may differ by device and app version.
