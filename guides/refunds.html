Refunds & adjustments

Where to review refund requests, credits and order adjustments. Follow the general steps below to find the right next step for your question.

What to do

Review the receipt
Open the order receipt and identify the charge or item adjustment you want to ask about.
Check the order's help options
Select the relevant order and look for the available issue or refund options.
Submit the details
Describe the issue accurately and attach supporting information if the official flow requests it.
Follow the status
Check your account or payment statement for updates. Processing times can vary by payment method and provider.
Only the retailer and your payment provider can confirm the status of a specific refund or adjustment.
