Order tracking & status

Understand order progress, delivery estimates and tracking updates. Follow the general steps below to find the right next step for your question.

What to do

Open your order details
Sign in to the Instacart app or website and select the order you want to review.
Review the latest status
Look for the current order stage, estimated arrival window and any messages or status updates.
Check delivery instructions
Review the address, drop-off instructions and any notes saved for the order.
If the status looks incorrect
Refresh the order page and check for a newer update. If the details still do not match what happened, contact Instacart through its official Help Center.
